Welcome to FRATAM Junior School

At FRATAM, we live by our motto: "Excelling is our goal." We believe every student is capable of greatness when provided with the right support, discipline, and encouragement.

Our junior school curriculum is carefully structured to transition young minds seamlessly through their primary academic years while maintaining strong ethical foundations.

Our Vision & Mission

Our direction is guided by a clear vision and a strong commitment to developing learners who can make a positive difference in society.

👁

Our Vision

To be a leading junior school that inspires learners to achieve academic excellence, develop strong character, discover their talents and become confident, responsible and productive members of society.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide quality, holistic and learner-centred education in a safe, supportive and inspiring environment where every child is encouraged to learn, grow, create, lead and excel academically, socially and morally.

Message from the Headteacher

Dear Parents, Guardians and Learners,
