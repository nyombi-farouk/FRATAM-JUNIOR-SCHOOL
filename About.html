Welcome to FRATAM Junior School

At FRATAM, we live by our motto: "Excelling is our goal." We believe every student is capable of greatness when provided with the right support, discipline, and encouragement.

Our junior school curriculum is carefully structured to transition young minds seamlessly through their primary academic years while maintaining strong ethical foundations.

Core values

Excellence:

We strive for high standards in academics, character, talents, and every area of school life.

Discipline:

We encourage self-control, responsibility, respect, and positive behavior.

Integrity:

We promote honesty, truthfulness, fairness, and doing the right thing even when no one is watching.

Respect:

We value every learner, teacher, parent, and member of our community.

Creativity & Innovation:

We encourage learners to think critically, explore new ideas, solve problems, and express their talents.

Responsibility:

We teach learners to take ownership of their actions, studies, relationships, and environment.

Confidence:

We nurture learners who believe in themselves and have the courage to participate, lead, and overcome challenges.

Teamwork:

We encourage cooperation, sharing, communication, and working together toward common goals.

Hard Work & Perseverance:

We believe that determination, commitment, and consistent effort are essential to achieving success.

Compassion & Kindness:

We encourage learners to care for others, support one another, and contribute positively to society.
